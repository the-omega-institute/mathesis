# §30. Published Lean evidence in the reader

Part of the repository contract in [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md); equally binding.

An internal preparation state is not evidence that a published paper lacks Lean
formalization. Display its exact-version pinned public Lean source in the graph,
paper, result reader and directory. Source nodes use filled squares and preserve
the source's original attribution; checked exact propositions use filled circles.
A manuscript statement whose precise declaration correspondence is not yet known
stays an unfilled result, described as having an available paper-level source
rather than a missing proof. Source-backed results without a confirmed proof
target do not enter the proof-opportunity filter. Source publication, target
preparation, independent receipt and newly earned contribution credit remain
separate. No source-file link checks every extracted statement or creates a
proof-use arrow, fresh score, model task or re-review. Never borrow a newer
paper version's source for an older result.
